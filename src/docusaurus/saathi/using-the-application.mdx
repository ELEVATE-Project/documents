# About Saathi

Saathi is built for school leaders and teachers who are trying to improve outcomes in their schools
and classrooms but need structured, contextual support to move from challenge to action. 




# Use Cases
Saathi is not a single-purpose planning tool. It is a flexible improvement companion that can
support a range of school and classroom needs including but not limited to:

    <td>Reduce school dropout rates</td>
    <td>When a school teacher observes a decline in attendance within a class, Saathi can be used as a support tool to help identify possible causes and develop strategies for improvement. By responding to a few simple, targeted questions, the teacher can provide the necessary context for Saathi to generate a structured 30–35 day micro-improvement plan, consisting of clear, practical, and actionable steps.
The teacher can then implement the plan in collaboration with the relevant team, monitor progress regularly, and make adjustments based on the outcomes observed throughout the period.</td>

<tr>
    <td>Effective action/lesson plans for slow learners</td>
    <td>A maths teacher is finding it challenging to make the children understand the concept of fractions. The teacher can use Saathi to describe the challenge
and seek guidance. Saathi can suggest a  30–35 day improvement plan with  small, practical actions that the teacher can implement in the classroom.. The teacher can then ask Saathi for detailed lesson plans or activity ideas to support the implementation of the plan and get that contextual support.</td>

 </td>
