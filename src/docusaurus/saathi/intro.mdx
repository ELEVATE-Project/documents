# Welcome to Saathi

Saathi is an AI-driven, multilingual, voice-enabled, open-source capability designed to improve the quality of education
in communities with low levels of literacy, or digital exposure, or technological limitations. 




## Overview

This microsite contains detailed documentation that helps school leaders and teachers understand how Saathi user concerns into structured plans that are
low-lift, actionable steps based on the globally tested frameworks of micro-improvement planning.


## Introduction

Across schools and communities, leaders and teachers encounter challenges every day.
A school leader sees declining student attendance, weak parent participation, or low teacher motivation.
A teacher notices that students are disengaged, unable to grasp a concept, or not progressing as expected. In most cases, the challenge is visible long before a solution is clear.

What is missing is not intent. It is the ability to identify the core issue and plan the action steps that are easy to implement.
Most existing support systems are either too generic, too technical, or too far away from the reality of everyday school life.
And above all what is missing is a guided thought partner that can work with a leader or teacher in their own context, around their own problem, at the moment they need help. 

The result is a gap in action. People closest to the challenge often know that something needs to change, but they do not always have the time, confidence, or structured support to translate that challenge into a clear, contextualized, and doable improvement plan. 
Saathi helps the user clarify the problem, understand what is within their locus of control, and identifies a practical action plan for solving the issue.

Saathi is an AI-powered companion that helps school leaders and teachers move from a visible challenge to a contextualized micro improvement plan through guided, conversational support.

For example: A school leader or teacher describes a challenge, question, or area where they need support.
Saathi engages them in a structured conversation, helps them reflect on the root issue, and guides them toward a focused micro improvement plan or a clearer understanding of the concept they are grappling with.
