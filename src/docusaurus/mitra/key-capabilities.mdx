# Key Capabilities of MItra

MItra can be used across sectors to capture improvements and discussions, and collect feedback and perceptions through voice based interactions.
From farmers and health workers to youth and community groups, MItra captures real stories of change from diverse contexts.

For people with low literacy or digital exposure, MItra is a
highly inclusive and accessible platform, as it enables users to interact with it in multiple
Indian languages.


The key capabilities of MItra are as follows:


<table>
<tr>
    <th>Features</th>
    <th>Description</th>

<tr>
    <td>AI-powered workflows</td>
    <td>MItra listens and transcribes conversations into structured knowledge that can be analysed, shared, and acted upon.
    MItra spots patterns, aggregates insights, and flags problems automatically without needing someone to manually comb through notes or recordings.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
    <td>Multilingual Support</td>
    <td>Enables users to interact with it in multiple Indian languages. Thus, making it easy for people to simply speak, narrate, and share their stories in their languages.
 </td>
</tr>
<tr>
    <td>Voice-first experience</td>
    <td>Uses voice-first technology, enabling users to speak in their local dialect.
MItra listens, transcribes, and converts that raw conversation into a clean structured format. </td>
</tr>
<tr>
    <td>Progressive Web App</td>
    <td>MItra is built as a Progressive Web App (PWA), enabling it to run seamlessly across any browsers and devices even with low internet bandwidth or mobile data.</td>
</tr>

<tr>
    <td>Sharable reports</td>
    <td>Generates enriched reports with discussion summaries or improvement stories along with photos.</td>
</tr>
<tr>

</td>
