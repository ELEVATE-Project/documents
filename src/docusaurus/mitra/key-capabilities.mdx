# Key Capabilities of MItra

MItra is used to collect micro-improvement stories from the ground, helping users easily document the challenges they addressed, the actions they took, and the changes they achieved.
It guides them to capture their story clearly and turns their narration into a well-structured report that can be shared to spread the learning.

For people with low literacy or digital exposure, MItra is a
highly inclusive and accessible platform, as it enables users to interact with it in multiple
Indian languages.
