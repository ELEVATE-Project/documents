
# About MItra 


When a user narrates a discussion in the community, or an improvement story, MItra listens and processes the voice- based or text-based inputs and creates a meaningful output with photos that can be shared to different stakeholders.
MItra separates the challenges and solutions and also identifies gaps in the narratives and converts everything into a clean structured format.

The key features of MItra include:

1. An intuitive interface for users to collect valuable data and insights in multiple Indian languages.

2. Enriched reports with discussion summaries or improvement stories along with photos.

3. Ability to process voice-based or text-based narratives, identifies the gaps in the narratives, isolates the challenges and solutions, and create a well-documented output in their preferred language.

4. Accessible to everyone who wishes to share their discussions and results from the chaupal,
    along with how it has contributed to the betterment of their communities in their preferred language.

5. Ability to prompt users to provide additional information where needed. It creates summaries that are accurate, contextual, and meaningful in a structured format.
