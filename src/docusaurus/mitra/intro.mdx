# Welcome to MItra

MItra is an AI-powered, multilingual, voice-enabled, open-source capability designed to listen at scale,
capture community discussions and micro-improvement stories, and gather feedback.

MItra captures community discussions and innovations at scale, bringing local improvements into focus.


## Overview

This microsite contains detailed documentation that helps organizations understand how MItra transforms raw,
unstructured narratives into usable reports, dashboards, and insights.

## Introduction

Various sectors regularly hold local discussions aimed at improving their communities so that everyone can live better lives. However, while these conversations and success stories may create meaningful impact locally, many of them remain unheard beyond their immediate surroundings.

The main challenge arises from low literacy levels, which may limit people’s ability to read or write, as well as the lack of effective communication channels to share discussions, identified challenges, actions taken, and outcomes achieved.

MItra addresses this gap by capturing improvement stories, community voices, feedback emerged from the meetings or discussions, impact assessments, and visible results, ensuring that these important efforts are properly documented and shared.

MItra is used by health workers, farmers, women, youth, and local community members to capture voices from the ground, recording real stories, everyday challenges, and the micro-improvement actions taken to address them. This ensures that grassroots perspectives are not lost but captured as authentic stories of change that others can see, learn from, and build upon.
