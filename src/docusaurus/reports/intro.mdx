# Welcome to Reports

Elevate Data is a crucial building block in the Elevate repository, designed to provide robust data visualization and reporting capabilities for data collected through other Elevate capabilities, namely  Projects, Mentor, Observations, and Surveys.
Elevate Data focuses on transforming this data into actionable insights in the form of dashboards and visualizations, enabling stakeholders such as program managers, state admins, district officials and session managers to make informed decisions.

## Overview

Elevate Data Reports are implemented as a reference framework configured and managed by users in the Super Admin role, featuring Admin, Program, State, and District dashboards.
These dashboards deliver comprehensive insights tailored to the needs of various stakeholders. While this implementation focuses on the education domain, the dashboards can be customized and adapted for other sectors, such as healthcare, to meet their unique requirements.
